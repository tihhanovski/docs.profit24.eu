# Getting started with Profit

Follow these simple steps to begin working with Profit:

- [Register your user profile](register.md)

    Creating a Profit ERP profile is free. We even credit your account so you can explore the system and start real work without any upfront commitment.

- [Choose your username](register.md#kasutajanime-valik)
- [Create a company database](create-db.md) or [request access to an existing company](access-other-company.md)
- [Fill in the most important company settings](primary-settings.md)
- [Adjust your personal user settings](user-settings.md)

That’s it! You can now issue sales invoices, enter purchase bills, manage inventory, and much more.
